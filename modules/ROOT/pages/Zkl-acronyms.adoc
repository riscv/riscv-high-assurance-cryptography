////
NOTE for the reviewer:
ignore acronyms RVWMO, RVTSO
Acronyms common in the RISC-V specifications, and very common ones, are commented out of the table.
KLE for "Cryptographic Lockers Extension" is ok, as "K" can stand for Cryptography
like in all other RISC-V cryptography extensions.
