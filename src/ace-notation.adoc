[[ACE-pseudocode-notation]]
== Pseudocode Notation

The symbols below are used throughout this specification. The representation of the
values they operate on, the meaning of the concatenation operator `@`, and the
finite-field operations `Galoismul`, `Montmul`, `update_mask` and `double` are fixed
normatively in <<ACE-conventions>>.

=== Operators and Control Constructs

* `x {leftarrow} y`, denotes assignment of `y` to `x`. The two spellings are
  synonymous.
* `x = y` and `x {ne} y` denote equality and inequality; `<`, `{le}`, `>`, `{ge}` denote
  the usual comparisons on integers.
* `+`, `-`, `{times}` denote integer addition, subtraction and multiplication.
  `a mod b`, for `b` > 0, denotes the remainder of the division of `a` by `b`, in the
  range `0` to `b-1`.
* `V xor W`, `V and W`, `V or W` denote bitwise operations on values of equal length,
  and `V << k`, `V >> k` the shifts defined in <<ACE-conventions>>.
* `A @ B` denotes concatenation, defined in <<ACE-conventions>>.
* `foreach(i from a to b) { {ellipsis} }` executes its body once for each integer `i`
  from `a` to `b`, both included, in increasing order; the body is not executed if
  `a` > `b`. The variant `foreach(i from a to b by s)` advances `i` in steps of `s`.
* `x^k^` denotes `x` raised to the power `k`. Inside listing blocks, where superscripts
  cannot be typeset, the same quantity is written `x**k`.
* `local x : T` declares an internal variable of the pseudocode that is not part of any
  architectural state.

=== Functions

* `0` denotes a single bit equal to zero.
* `1` denotes a single bit equal to one.
* `bin(n,m)` = binary representation of `n` to `m` bits (`m` least significant bits, or sign extended). The representations is little endian.
* `binBE(n,m)` = binary representation of `n` to `m` bits (`m` least significant bits, or sign extended). The representations is byte-wise big-endian. In other words, when written to memory starting at byte address `X`, the most significant byte of `binBE(n,m)` is stored at byte address `X`, the second most significant byte is stored at byte address `X+1`, and so on.
* `bswap(m)` = the octet string `m` with its octet order reversed. See <<ACE-conventions>>.
* `ceil(x)` = the smallest integer not smaller than `x`; also written `{lceil}x{rceil}`.
* `double(S)` = doubling in the OCB3 field. See <<ACE-conventions-fields>>.
* `floor(x)` = the largest integer not larger than `x`; also written `{lfloor}x{rfloor}`.
* `Galoismul(a, b)` = multiplication of `a` and `b` in the GHASH field. See <<ACE-conventions-fields>>.
* `int(m)` = the unsigned integer represented by `m`. See <<ACE-conventions>>.
* `len_in_bits(M)` = the length of the value or string `M` in bits.
* `len_in_bytes(M)` = the length of the value or string `M` in bytes, i.e.,
  `len_in_bits(M) / 8`. It is defined only when `len_in_bits(M)` is a multiple of 8.
* `lsb(x)` = least significant bit of `x`, and `lsb~c~(x)` its `c` least significant bits.
* `m[i .. j]` = bytes from the ``i``-th to the ``j``-th of byte array ``m``.
* `m[j:i]` = bit-slice from the ``i``-th to the ``j``-th bit of ``m``.
* `min(x, y, {ellipsis})`, `max(x, y, {ellipsis})` = the smallest, resp. largest, of
  their arguments.
* `Montmul(a, b)` = the POLYVAL field operation. See <<ACE-conventions-fields>>.
* `msb(x)` = most significant bit of `x`, and `msb~c~(x)` its `c` most significant bits.
* `ones(i)` = sequence of `i` ones.
* `update_mask(M)` = the XEX/XTS mask update. See <<ACE-conventions-fields>>.
* `zeros(i)` = sequence of `i` zeros.
