[[ACE-new-names]]
== Proposal for Ratified Names

* While *Algorithm* is suggestive, it is not a precise name for what a Cryptographic Context does.
 I propose to change the name to *Machine* (as in State Machine). +
 So: a *Cryptographic Context* described the state of a *Machine*, and it is operative when stored into a *Cryptographic Locker*.
 It can be exported as a *Sealed Cryptographic Context*.

* ACE would use a sub-domain of the Cryptography extensions, `k`.
Since the sub-domain `l` is currently not allocated, we would use “`kl`” as a prefix in place of “`ace.`”.

* `kl` can also be read as an abbreviation of “Cryptographic Lockers”, which is an apt name.

* We probably need a name change anyway since the acronym ACE has been overloaded over the two years of ACE development, namely
 Intel's “AI Compute Extension”; and
 IBM's Assured confidential execution for RISC-V.

* New instruction names (two proposals):
+
--
[float="center",align="center",cols="^6,<18,<18,<18,<40",options="header"]
|===
|   #   | Old Name        | New Name (1)   | New Name (2) | Meaning
|   1   | `ace.load`      | `kl.load`      | `kll`        | Load content into a CR from memory
|   2   | `ace.store`     | `kl.store`     | `kls`        | Store content from a CR to memory
|   3   | `ace.exec`      | `kl.exec`      | `klx`        | Execute Operation
|   4   | `ace.mv`        | `kl.mv`        | `klmv`       | Move data in/out of a CR
|   5   | `ace.setst`     | `kl.setst`     | `klss`       | Set CR State
|  --   | `ace.mgmt`      | `kl.mgmt`      | `klmg`       | Manage a CR's Contents
|  --   | `ace.clear`     | `kl.clear`     | `klclr`      | Clear a CR
|  --   | `ace.clear.all` | `kl.clear.all` | `klclrx`     | Clear all CRs and the ACEIOBUF
|  --   | `ace.clear.ads` | `kl.clear.ads` | `klclrads`   | Remove the ADS from the target CR
|   6   | `ace.getmdl`    | `kl.getmdl`    | `klgmdl`     | Get CR Metadata, low 64 bits, in GPRs
|  --   | `ace.getmd`     | `kl.getmd`     | `klgmd`      | Get CR Metadata, all 128 bits, in GPRs
|  --   | `ace.getmdv`    | `kl.getmdv`    | `klgmd.v`    | Get CR Metadata, all 128 bits, in a Vector
|  --   | `ace.getst`     | `kl.getst`     | `klgst`      | Get State from a CR
|  --   | `ace.getstx`    | `kl.getstex`   | `klgstx`     | Get State Extension Field from a CR
|   7   | `ace.restrictl` | `kl.restrictl` | `klrsl`      | Restrict a CR's policies in the Low 64 bits
|  --   | `ace.restricth` | `kl.restricth` | `klrs`       | Restrict a CR's policies in all 128 bits
|  --   | `ace.restrictv` | `kl.restrictv` | `klrs.v`     | --- Vector register version
|   8   | `ace.clone`     | `kl.clone`     | `klcp`       | Clone a CR
|   9   | `ace.derive`    | `kl.derive`    | `kld`        | Derive material from a CR into another
|  10   | `ace.size`      | `kl.size`      | `kllen`      | Get SCC Size
|  --   | `ace.csize`     | `kl.csize`     | `klclen`     | Get Content Size
|  --   | `ace.avail`     | `kl.avail`     | `klck`       | Check Availability of an Algorithm
|  11   | `ace.input`     | `kl.input`     | `klin`       | Input data into the ACEIOBUF
|  12   | `ace.output`    | `kl.output`    | `klout`      | Output data into the ACEIOBUF
|===
--

* For all states and other names, including CSRs, `ace_` becomes `kl_`, and
`mace_` becomes `mkl_`, ACEIOBUF becomes KLIO/KLIOBUF, etc.
